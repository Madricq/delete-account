We respect your privacy and give users the ability to request deletion of their accounts and associated personal information.

1. How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion, send an email to our support address using the email address associated with your account.

Include the following information:

  • Your registered email address.
  • Your account or business name, if applicable.
  • A clear statement that you want your account deleted.
Request Account Deletion

2. What Information Is Deleted?

Once a deletion request has been verified and processed, we will delete or anonymize personal information associated with your account where applicable, including:

  • Account profile information.
  • Business profile and receptionist settings.
  • Call histories and associated metadata.
  • Stored transcripts, summaries and messages.
  • Other personal information associated with your account.

3. Information That May Be Retained

Certain information may need to be retained where required by law, for legitimate security purposes, fraud prevention, financial recordkeeping or dispute resolution.

Retained information will be limited to what is reasonably necessary and will be handled according to our Privacy Policy.

4. How Long Does Deletion Take?

We aim to review and process verified deletion requests within 30 days.

Where additional time is legally permitted and necessary, we will communicate the reason for the delay.

5. Does Deletion Cancel All Services?

Deleting your account may disable access to the application and its associated services.

If you have configured call forwarding through your mobile network operator or another telecommunications provider, you may need to disable that forwarding separately. Deleting your App account does not automatically guarantee that carrier-side forwarding settings are cancelled.
